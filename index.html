The idea

Every project is two lists

Built and working

Buys trust. They cannot see the work, so they assume nothing is happening.

Waiting on you

Only they can supply it. Photos, a number, prices, permission, their story.

The idea

Why one list fails

A mixed list reads
as a status update.

So they do nothing. Two lists, the second addressed to them, reads as a job.

The discipline

Grep first.
Model second.

The regex pass is free, instant, and never lies. Hand the model the results and ask it to phrase them for someone who does not code.

The build

Voice

Write to them,
not about them

"Your phone number", never "client phone number".

  • Every ask gets a reason
  • No jargon without a plain sentence beside it
  • Never make them feel late
  • It reads as a plan, not a chase
The build

The page that does the work

The shot list

Tell a non photographer exactly what to stand where and photograph. Six to ten items. This is what actually unblocks projects.

  • The walled garden, wide, whole wall in frame
  • The same garden close up, stonework readable
  • A wall you repaired, flat or cloudy light
  • The team mid job, not posed
  • One before and one after, same spot
